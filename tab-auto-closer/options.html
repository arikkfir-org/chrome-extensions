Tab Auto-Closer

Closes a tab whose address matches one of the patterns below, after a delay. A banner on the page counts down and lets you keep the tab open.

0 closes the tab as soon as it loads, without a banner.

Chrome match patterns, such as https://*.zoom.us/j/*. *.example.com also matches example.com. Saving asks Chrome for access to each pattern's site, and gives up access to sites no pattern needs anymore.
